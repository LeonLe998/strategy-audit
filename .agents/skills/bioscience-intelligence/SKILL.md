# Bioscience Intelligence & Computational Biology Skill

## Overview
This skill provides access to and guidance on utilizing bioscience and biotechnology datasets for structural biology, molecular medicine, and quantitative biotech pipeline evaluation.

## Key Bioscience Skill Capabilities
1. **AlphaFold Protein Prediction**:
   - Query UniProt accession IDs to inspect predicted 3D structures and pLDDT confidence scores.
   - Categorize residues into very high confidence (>90), confident (70-90), low (50-70), and disordered (<50).

2. **ChEMBL Bioactivity & Drug Targets**:
   - Query bioactive molecules, IC50 / Ki values, and target mechanisms of action.

3. **Clinical Trials & Pipeline Intelligence**:
   - Track Phase 1, Phase 2, and Phase 3 trial milestones, endpoints, and patient cohorts on ClinicalTrials.gov.

4. **PubChem Chemical Profiler**:
   - Fetch 2D/3D molecular conformations, SMILES, and physicochemical descriptors.

5. **3D Molecular Visualization**:
   - Render interactive DNA double helices and protein macromolecules in real-time WebGL.
