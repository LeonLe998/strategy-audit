## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the clinical rigor, mathematical transparency, and calm precision demanded by quantitative finance and algorithmic strategy verification. It consciously departs from retail trading hyperbole, gamified dopamine triggers, and casino aesthetics. The experience is designed to project the authority of institutional research desks, risk auditing suites, and empirical laboratory platforms.

### Target Audience
- Quantitative researchers, systematic traders, algorithmic portfolio architects, and institutional risk officers.
- Disciplined individual operators who prioritize edge verification, maximum drawdown mitigation, statistical sample size, and mathematical expectancy over speculative intuition.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern High-Density Analytical Precision
The aesthetic blends **Dark Precision Minimalist UI** with **Technical Subsurface Elevation**:
- **Surgical Precision**: Razor-thin structural borders, precise 1px hairline boundaries, tabular alignments, and unambiguous metadata hierarchies.
- **Controlled Optical Restraint**: Zero neon lens flares or faux-futuristic glow clutter. Contrast is strictly engineered for low-fatigue multi-screen analytical workflows.
- **Empirical Clarity**: State changes and backtest outcomes rely on dual-encoding (explicit indicators, numerical deltas, directional labels, and signed figures) rather than mere color shifts.

## Colors

### Canvas & Surface Architecture
- **Root Background (`#0B0F17`)**: Deep Obsidian Navy anchor providing a non-fatiguing, deep base for extended backtest evaluations.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#111827`)**: Primary card panels, layout containers, and metrics sidebars.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#1A2234`)**: Floating inspection cards, data table rows on active states, tab bars, and module headers.
- **Hairline Structural Borders (`#2A364F`)**: Calibrated 1px boundary dividing telemetry cards and control blocks with crisp architectural rhythm.

### Semantic Data Signals (Research & Risk Telemetry)
- **Primary Metric Accent (`#10B981` Emerald)**: Verification passes, positive alpha, benchmark outperformance, profit factor thresholds (> 1.5).
- **Secondary Discovery Accent (`#06B6D4` Cyan Tech)**: Execution triggers, active telemetry markers, audit progression indicators, and benchmark overlay series.
- **Risk & Drawdown Warning (`#F43F5E` Soft Coral/Rose)**: Max Drawdown (MDD) breaches, high-risk exposure, statistical anomalies, and invalidated hypotheses. Designed without speculative anxiety—grounded, informative, and definitive.
- **Neutral & Information Grid (`#94A3B8` Slate Neutral)**: Secondary axes, standard deviation bounds, labels, column headers, and structural grid guides.
- **Text & Foreground**: Primary headings set in `#F8FAFC`, secondary documentation in `#CBD5E1`, and structural meta-captions in `#64748B`.

## Typography

### Structural Role Assignment
- **Primary Interface & Editorial Flow (`Inter`)**: Deployed across UI controls, navigational structures, research findings, and structural headers. Rendered with calibrated negative letter-spacing at scale to project technical authority and modern industrial polish.
- **Telemetry & Quantitative Figures (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular figures (`tnum`) applied across all numerical readouts, equity timestamps, PnL ratios, Sharpe/Sortino parameters, trade logs, and delta badges. This eliminates horizontal jitter across real-time dynamic audits.

### Visual Data Rules
- Every numerical value indicating backtest performance (e.g., win rate, profit factor, drawdown) must explicitly include sign notation (`+` or `-`) and unit denominators (`%`, `R`, `pts`).
- Header hierarchies avoid decorative stylistic flourishes; weight and proportional tracking establish immediate focus.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (12-Column Fluid Grid)**: Max canvas width of 1600px centered, with 20px (`1.25rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) screen margins. Supports multi-column telemetry: left-rail parameter controls (3 cols), central equity visualization (6 cols), and right-rail risk inspection metrics (3 cols).
- **Tablet (8-Column Responsive)**: Collapses secondary parameters into collapsible drawers, splitting the main viewport between equity performance and statistical summaries.
- **Mobile (4-Column Stacked)**: Streamlines audit stages and critical performance figures into vertically serialized inspection panels with 12px gutters.

### Linear Rhythms & Gaps
- Spacing follows an analytical 4px baseline matrix. Micro-spacings (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) are strictly enforced within metric chip clusters, tabular cells, and dual-label rows. Structural distances (`space-lg`, `space-xl`) segment disparate analytical domains (e.g., Stage 1: Hypothesis Formulation vs. Stage 2: Empirical Stress Test).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is maintained via tonal stacking and 1px crisp separation rather than heavy skeuomorphic cast shadows:

1. **Base Floor (`#0B0F17`)**: The infinite data canvas; pure dark matte.
2. **Structural Paneling (`#111827`)**: Sub-surface containers framed with a solid 1px border (`#2A364F`). Shadows are entirely absent or limited to a resting containment floor (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
3. **Floating Overlays & Auditing Inspect Tooltips (`#1A2234`)**:
   - Elevated using subtle optical depth: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px #2A364F`.
   - Subtle backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) at 90% opacity to retain data context underneath dynamic inspection points.
4. **Active/Focus States**: Outlined by a crisp 1px highlight (`#06B6D4` or `#10B981`) with zero diffuse ambient glow, keeping vector edges sharp across high-DPI displays.

## Shapes

### Edge Precision (`Soft - Level 1`)
- Standard input controls, cards, structural metric modules, and data cells use a disciplined `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- System dialogs, modal audit confirmations, and parent panels adopt `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- Micro status tags, pill indicators, and table-value badges maintain subtle chamfered bounds rather than full semicircles. This sharp geometry reinforces programmatic, software-driven utility over casual consumer styling.

## Components

### 1. Verification Buttons & CTA Controls
- **Primary Action (Run Audit / Export Quant Log)**: Deep solid `#10B981` background, `#0B0F17` ultra-dark high-contrast typography, 4px border radius, font weight 600. Hover transitions to `#059669` without glow effects.
- **Secondary Action (Parameter Adjust / Compare Benchmark)**: Transparent surface framed in a 1px border (`#2A364F`), `#F8FAFC` typography. Hover shifts fill to `#1A2234` with boundary highlight in `#06B6D4`.
- **Destructive / Reset Action**: Bordered in `#F43F5E` at 30% alpha with matching muted rose label text.

### 2. Metric & Telemetry Badges (Chips)
- Structured as unified pairs: `<Key Label>` in `#94A3B8` adjacent to `<Tabular Value>` in bold monospace (`JetBrains Mono`).
- Positive Alpha Badge: Fill `#10B981` at 12% opacity, 1px border at 30% `#10B981`, text `#10B981`. Icon prefix: `▲ +[Value]%`.
- Drawdown Stress Badge: Fill `#F43F5E` at 12% opacity, 1px border at 30% `#F43F5E`, text `#F43F5E`. Icon prefix: `▼ -[Value]%`.

### 3. Equity Curve & Drawdown Chart Module
- **Primary Equity Trace**: Crisp 2px continuous line in `#10B981`. Area under curve populated with a linear downward gradient transitioning from `#10B981` (10% opacity) to completely transparent at baseline.
- **Underwater / Drawdown Sub-Chart**: Dedicated synchronized pane below equity. Negative area filled with `#F43F5E` (15% opacity) and 1px edge stroke in `#F43F5E`.
- **Benchmark Trajectory**: 1px dashed reference line in `#64748B`.
- **Crosshair Hover Inspection**: Hairline 1px dash `#06B6D4`. Popover displays tabular date, current balance, trade index, and delta to high-water mark.

### 4. Pedagogical Workflow: LEARN -> MEASURE -> PRACTICE (HỌC -> ĐO -> THỰC HÀNH)
- Horizontal 3-node segmented pipeline with 1px border `#2A364F` and `#111827` surface:
  - **Node 1: LEARN (HỌC)**: Strategy logic specifications, edge thesis, and risk rules definitions.
  - **Node 2: MEASURE (ĐO)**: Monte Carlo distribution, Sharpe, Profit Factor, sample size validation, and out-of-sample backtest telemetry.
  - **Node 3: PRACTICE (THỰC HÀNH)**: Forward paper execution, position sizing calculator, and disciplined playbook compliance checklist.
- Active stage is indicated by a `#06B6D4` bottom accent rail (2px) and high-contrast `#F8FAFC` title. Completed stages feature an `#10B981` check indicator. Upcoming stages remain muted in `#64748B`.

### 5. Input Fields & Parameter Steppers
- Dark container fill `#0B0F17`, 1px boundary `#2A364F`.
- Inline labels top-aligned in `Inter` 11px uppercase (`#94A3B8`).
- Numeric parameter input typed in `JetBrains Mono` 14px with integrated micro increment/decrement steppers.

### 6. Strategy Cards & Risk Matrix Table
- Strategy Cards display status chips, strategy moniker, mathematical expectancy metric, and a 60px wide sparkline showing historical drawdown profile.
- Tables feature sticky `#111827` headers with 1px bottom divider, monospace numerical cells right-aligned, and hover rows styled in `#1A2234`.
